A small firm with a long view.

Who we are

Sirius Capital is an investment firm focused on putting capital to work for the long term. We keep the firm small on purpose, so the people who make the decisions are the people you talk to.

We would rather own a few things we understand deeply than many things we only follow.

Our approach

We start by writing down why we want to own something and what would change our mind. We return to that note as facts change, so our decisions stay tied to evidence rather than mood.

Understand first

We invest only where we can explain how the business makes money.

Think in years

We judge results over full cycles, not single quarters.

Say it plainly

Updates are written in clear language, with the numbers beside them.

Why Sirius

Sirius is the brightest star in the night sky. It is also two stars, a bright one and a faint one, held together by each other's pull.

We liked the idea. Good investing is rarely about the star that is easy to see. It is about understanding what holds the system together.
